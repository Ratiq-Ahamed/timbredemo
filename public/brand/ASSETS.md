# Brand asset

`timbrel-logo.png` was present in the workspace before implementation. It is a black “TL / Timbrel Tales” mark on white, supplied by the user. The user approved its unchanged use for this local demo while retaining the product name **Timbrel Studio OS**.

No extraction, tracing, recolouring or replacement was performed. `BrandMark` in `components/shell.tsx` is the shared rendering point. It preserves the complete image and aspect ratio on a neutral white panel; a text fallback handles load failure. Replace this one asset to update entry, workspaces and printable quotes.

Permission recorded here covers the local demo; public marketing approval remains a separate decision. Proposed product colour tokens do not claim to be official brand colours.
