# Timbrel Studio OS
### Every enquiry. Every shoot. Every asset. Every memory.
**A customized photography business operating system by Myurrix**

## Client-facing product description
Timbrel Studio OS is a proposed centralized workspace designed around the way Timbrel Studio manages weddings, portraits and other photography assignments. From incoming enquiries and customized quotations to multi-day bookings, freelancer coordination, equipment handovers, editing, albums, collections and delivery, it brings each stage of the business together in a connected system.

The owner gets visibility into upcoming events, unfilled crew roles, equipment custody, follow-ups, delivery deadlines and outstanding payments. Freelancers receive a dedicated mobile workspace for assignments, equipment acknowledgements, handover updates and payout history. Customers receive a branded, private experience for tracking their event, reviewing previews, selecting album photographs and receiving eligible final files, with physical delivery still available when preferred.

With optional authorized Meta campaign and lead integrations, the studio can connect advertising enquiries to actual bookings and measure marketing performance where attribution is available. Itemized crew and production expenses enable project-level contribution reporting, helping the owner understand the economics of each assignment. The goal is less manual coordination, stronger accountability and a more consistent customer experience—not replacement of tools that already work well.

| Event operations | Plan all sessions and people | Schedule a multi-day wedding; identify a crew/gear conflict |

You already invest in generating enquiries, photography equipment and freelance teams. Timbrel Studio OS is designed to connect those investments to a single operating system. It lets you follow every booking from enquiry to delivery, check which photographer has each camera, assign teams without conflicting schedules, monitor editing and album deadlines, track collections and freelancer payments, and understand the contribution from each event. Your clients can receive a premium branded online experience rather than depending solely on physical pen drives. We will tailor the system to your actual workflow and retain your existing website and gallery tools wherever they make sense.

## Product design personality
**Editorial warmth + operational precision.** Think of a refined photography studio's printed album combined with a professional business console. The admin experience should be structured and fast, not a decorative portfolio. The customer experience should foreground imagery and feel personal. The freelancer experience should prioritize immediate mobile actions. All three share brand, typography and semantic design tokens. The proposed warm charcoal/ivory palette in the master prompt is a direction, not a claim about the studio's official identity.

## Commercial guardrails
Do not promise a savings percentage, actual profitability, unlimited storage, guaranteed conversion improvement, automated ad retrieval, full accounting compliance, biometric functionality or live payment processing before validating dependencies. Use a discovery-led implementation with phases, accepted scope and quotas. Compare the existing Pixieset subscription and competing studio systems before proposing replacement. The sample data and names in the demo are fictitious. Use approved logo and photo assets; never publicly repurpose private client imagery without permission.
