## Useful secondary checks

- Draft gallery Arjun & Nila: simulate interrupted upload, attempt publish, retry the failed photo and publish successfully. Customer C-002 cannot view C-003's gallery.
- Gallery access settings: simulate expiry/revocation and preview the restricted customer state. Restore through reset.
- Lead import: paste a small CSV, map columns, review rows and import. Duplicate contact/error rows are reported; no automatic merge occurs.
- Reschedule: preview new session times; any conflict aborts all changes. Cancellation preserves open gear custody and financial history and opens a refund review.
- Settings allows another freelancer/customer identity for demonstrations. The selected role is a simulation, not production access security.
